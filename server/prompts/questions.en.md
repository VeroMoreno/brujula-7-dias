# Brújula · 7 days — guided questions (EN)

> A starting point, not a script. You can rewrite the question of the day if a
> different one matters more to you.

## 1
What transition are you going through, and what set it off?

## 2
What are you losing in this change? Name it without softening it.

## 3
What might you gain — even if it isn't clear yet?

## 4
What scares you about moving forward? And about not moving?

## 5
If this went well, what would your daily life look like six months from now?

## 6
What belief about yourself or the world is being challenged?

## 7
Look back at the previous six days. What pattern do you see? What small next step feels honest today?
